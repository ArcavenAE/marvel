# AGENTS.md

This file is the portable contract that Codex, Crush and OpenCode read.
`CLAUDE.md` carries the Claude additions; where the two overlap, this file
holds the rule every harness follows.

## PR and issue bodies

Text here posts under the maintainer's name, and reviewers pay for every body that hides why it matters, so these hold in every harness.

- Open each PR and issue body, and each comment that proposes, reports or asks, with one or two plain sentences: why read it, and why acting on it is worth the review time and the risk. Templates come after; never instead.
- Bug reports: the opener, then Expected (the contract or a precedent), Actual, the smallest decisive repro, the mechanism, and one question-form ask.
- Never write an em dash (U+2014), except in a separator slot a format prescribes (the kos action-commit separator). Never "comprehensive" in any form, "completely", "robust", "seamless", "cutting-edge", "best of breed", or other marketing language. No AI attribution. No "we" on the maintainer's behalf. At most one flourish per item.
- Before posting, sweep out unicode ellipses and smart quotes, "would be happy to", stacked hedges, rule-of-three lists, "It's worth noting", and a closing sentence that restates its paragraph.
