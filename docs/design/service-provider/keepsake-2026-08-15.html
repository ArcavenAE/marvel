Marvel as a Service Provider

A design session, played out in the round.

2026-08-14 / 2026-08-15 · BMAD party-mode

The house asked a single question: how can marvel hand services to agents and teams of agents without becoming the monolith that owns and trusts everything? What follows is who was in the room and what each of them pushed the design toward. The long-form record lives in the files beside this one; this page is the remembrance.

What the room converged on

The shape, in one breath

  • Planes are the outward seams (control, service, event, trust); fabrics are the inward wiring (the backplane, and its dual, the measurement fabric).
  • The backplane is the floor under what marvel already shipped: register, route, supervise, meter. The reconciler and the event ring are its first citizens.
  • The resource model splits and merges rather than grows: Casting, Binding, Projection, one Service with a provider field.
  • Services divide by lifecycle: runtime (bd, kos, flyloft, critic, code-graph, the bus) versus provisioning (sideshow, callbook, curtain).
  • The credential boundary: marvel brokers, never hoards; a minted attenuable identity is the one thing crossing the wall.
  • The proving vertical: bd ready, through a minted identity and one binding, returning go or no-go. Exercises every plane with nothing speculative in the parts.

Hard line, held all night: inference informs a plane, it never enforces on one. The caretaker proposes; a deterministic gate executes.

Speculative until proven. The design lives in README.md, 01-brief-prd.md, 02-architecture.md, and 03-probes-and-roadmap.md beside this page, and in the graph as question-marvel-service-provider-shape. The walk-ons were thanked and released; the roster was left as it was.
